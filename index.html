About

I’m Victor Stanovoy, founder and CEO of SparkNews™, and the creator of Pocket Union, a premium collection of thoughtfully made notebooks and travel accessories designed for life in motion. From the morning commute to journeys across the world, Pocket Union brings together refined design and everyday utility, creating objects made to carry, write, organise, and keep close. I believe the objects we carry should be as considered as the moments we use them for.
