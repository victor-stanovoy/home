About

I’m Victor Stanovoy, founder and CEO of SparkNews™, and the creator of Pocket Union, a premium collection of thoughtfully made notebooks and travel accessories designed for life in motion. From the morning commute to journeys across the world, Pocket Union brings together refined design and everyday utility, creating objects made to carry, write, organise, and keep close. I believe the objects we carry should be as considered as the moments we use them for.
Kind Regards,

        Victor Stanovoy is the founder and CEO of SparkNews™, a student newspaper that lives in more than one school. He is also the creator of Pocket Union™, a premium collection of thoughtfully made notebooks and travel accessories designed for life in motion. From the morning commute to journeys across the world, Pocket Union™ brings together refined design and everyday utility: objects made to carry, write, organise and keep close. Its notebooks are The Standard, The Meridian and The Commuter. Victor believes the objects we carry should be as considered as the moments we use them for, and he loves writing by hand.
